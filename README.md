I am a recent Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the **Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)** and currently a research intern at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**.

My current work examines how AI systems can construct and complete machine-checkable **TLA+ proofs**, and how such systems should be evaluated rigorously. More broadly, I am interested in formal verification, machine-assisted reasoning, dependable distributed systems, and software reliability.

## Research

- **Machine-assisted formal reasoning** — I contribute to [TLAPS-Bench](https://github.com/specula-org/tlaps-bench), a benchmark for evaluating AI systems on completing and constructing machine-checkable TLA+ proofs.
- **Dependable software systems** — I have worked on evaluating AI agents against realistic production failures, including failures that disrupt normal diagnostic access.
- **Bengali speech representations** — My undergraduate research studied where Bengali phone-like information emerges across Whisper encoder layers using speaker-disjoint probing and cross-model comparison.
